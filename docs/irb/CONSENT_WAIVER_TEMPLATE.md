**IRB Protocol**: [NUMBER]

**Study Title**: Validation of an Open-Source Cardiac AI Suite (cardio-echo-suite) for Echocardiography and ECG Pre-Reading at [INSTITUTION NAME]

### Criteria for Waiver (per 45 CFR 46.116(f))

The IRB may waive the requirement for informed consent if all of the following criteria are met. We address each below:

#### 1. The research involves no more than minimal risk to the subjects.

This study involves retrospective analysis of de-identified medical records. There is no direct patient contact, no intervention, and no additional testing. The only risk is breach of confidentiality, which is minimized by HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification, secured servers, and limited access. The risk is therefore minimal.

#### 2. The waiver will not adversely affect the rights and welfare of the subjects.

Because the data is de-identified per HIPAA Safe Harbor, subjects cannot be identified from the data. Their welfare is not affected — the study does not change their medical care, and the data is used only to validate AI accuracy. The right to privacy is protected by de-identification.

#### 3. The research could not practicably be carried out without the waiver.

To obtain individual consent from 300-500 patients whose echocardiograms and ECGs were performed between [START DATE] and [END DATE] would require:
- Locating patients, some of whom may have moved, died, or have dementia
- Contacting each patient (multiple attempts, mail/phone)
- Consent interviews (~30 minutes each)
- Total estimated time: 600-1000 hours of research coordinator time

This is not practicable for a retrospective validation study. The de-identification process further makes individual consent unnecessary because the study team cannot identify any individual subject.

#### 4. Whenever appropriate, the subjects will be provided with additional pertinent information after participation.

Not applicable. Because the data is de-identified, we cannot return to individual subjects with results. Results will be reported in aggregate. If significant findings emerge that could affect patient care, they will be communicated to [INSTITUTION NAME] clinical leadership for quality improvement.

#### 5. The research could not practicably be carried out without access to and use of the protected health information.

The study's purpose is to validate AI accuracy on real clinical data. Without access to echocardiogram and ECG data, the validation cannot be performed. De-identified data is sufficient for the analysis — no identifying information is needed.

### Conclusion

We request a full waiver of informed consent for this retrospective, de-identified, minimal-risk validation study. All HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers will be removed before the study team accesses the data.
