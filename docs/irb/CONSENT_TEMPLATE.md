### 1. Why am I being asked to take part in this research study?

You are being asked to take part in this study because you had an echocardiogram (heart ultrasound) or electrocardiogram (ECG, heart rhythm test) at [INSTITUTION NAME]. We are testing a computer program that uses artificial intelligence (AI) to help doctors read these tests faster.

### 2. What is the purpose of this study?

The purpose of this study is to test whether an AI computer program can accurately read echocardiograms and ECGs. The AI program was developed by researchers at Stanford, Yale, and other universities. We want to see if it works well on patients at [INSTITUTION NAME].

### 3. How many people will take part in this study?

About [300-500] people will take part in this study at [INSTITUTION NAME].

### 4. What will happen if I take part in this study?

If you agree to take part:
- We will use the echocardiogram and/or ECG that you already had. **No additional tests will be done.**
- We will remove your name, date of birth, and other identifying information from your test.
- We will run the AI program on your de-identified test.
- We will compare the AI's reading to your doctor's reading.
- We will store the results securely for 5 years, then destroy them.

This study does **not** change your medical care in any way. Your doctor will treat you exactly the same whether or not you participate.

### 5. How long will I be in this study?

This study uses your existing medical records. There are no study visits. Your data will be used once, then stored securely for 5 years.

### 6. What are the risks of this study?

- **Breach of confidentiality**: There is a small risk that someone could see your medical information. We minimize this risk by removing all identifying information from your records and storing them on secured, password-protected servers.
- **No physical risks**: This study does not involve any procedures, medications, or interventions.

### 7. What are the benefits of this study?

- **Direct benefit to you**: None. Your medical care will not change.
- **Benefit to others**: This study will help doctors learn whether AI can read heart tests accurately. If it works, future patients may get their test results faster.

### 8. What are my alternatives to taking part in this study?

You can choose not to participate. Your decision will not affect your medical care at [INSTITUTION NAME] in any way.

### 9. Will it cost me anything to take part?

No. There is no cost to you for taking part in this study.

### 10. Will I be paid to take part?

No. You will not be paid for taking part in this study.

### 11. What happens if I am injured by this study?

This study uses your existing medical records and does not involve any procedures. Therefore, there is no risk of physical injury from this study.

### 12. How will my privacy be protected?

- All identifying information (name, date of birth, medical record number, dates of service) will be removed from your records before they are used in this study.
- A "honest broker" at [INSTITUTION NAME] will handle the de-identification. The study team will not see your identifying information.
- De-identified data will be stored on secured, password-protected servers at [INSTITUTION NAME].
- Only the study team listed in the IRB protocol will have access to the data.
- Data will be destroyed 5 years after the study ends.
- Results of this study may be published in medical journals or shared at scientific meetings, but no information that could identify you will ever be published.

### 13. What are my rights as a study participant?

- Your participation is voluntary.
- You may choose not to take part, or you may withdraw at any time, without any penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.
- You will be told of any important new information that might affect your willingness to continue in the study.
- If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, you may contact the [INSTITUTION NAME] IRB at [IRB PHONE] or [IRB EMAIL].

### 14. Who can I contact if I have questions?

- **Study questions**: [PI NAME], [PI PHONE], [PI EMAIL]
- **IRB questions**: [INSTITUTION NAME] IRB, [IRB PHONE], [IRB EMAIL]
- **Patient advocate**: [INSTITUTION NAME] Patient Advocate, [PHONE]
